## The myth of the “expensive system”

A few years ago, having your own system meant physical servers, expensive licenses and a large team. That has changed.

Today we build on modern infrastructure where **cost follows usage**: it starts low and only grows when your business grows. You don’t pay for idle capacity.

## Case: KingFix, on-demand car repair in Canada

**KingFix** serves clients in Toronto with a mobile workshop that comes to the car. To grow, it needed clients to book on their own, with no phone calls and no manual price calculations.

We built the platform end to end:

1. **Online booking with automatic pricing.** The client picks a service, and the system calculates travel distance (Google Maps) and the price on the spot.
2. **Integrated payments (Stripe).** Money comes in without manual checking.
3. **Secure login (Clerk)**, with Google or email, like the big apps.
4. **Admin dashboard and financial management** that work on mobile.
5. **A fast, secure database (Neon)** for client data.

### The result

The platform is **live in Canada**, with the whole operation organized in one place and a low monthly cost. Clients book and pay on their own, and the company tracks everything from the dashboard. The system became a business **asset**, not another spreadsheet that depends on someone remembering to update it.
